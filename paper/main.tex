\begin{abstract}
As space-based photometric surveys like TESS produce millions of light curves, automated vetting via deep learning has become essential. However, the "black-box" nature of monolithic neural networks limits their adoption by astrophysicists who require explainable physical heuristics. We present AstroVerse, an open-source research platform introducing an explainable Evolutionary Mixture-of-Experts (EvoMoE) architecture. EvoMoE dynamically routes transit signals across morphological (CNN), temporal (Transformer), and physical (MLP) experts. We rigorously benchmark this approach against classical and statistical methods using a curated, multi-source dataset spanning the TESS Input Catalog (TIC) and Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (MAST).
\end{abstract}
