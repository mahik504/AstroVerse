\section{Results}
The numbers that belong with this system are the parameter count, the CPU latency, and the photometry of the reference library. They are reported in the manuscript at \texttt{paper/scie/AstroVerse\_SCIE\_manuscript.pdf}: 1{,}485{,}605 trainable parameters, a median forward-pass time of 7.64\,ms on CPU, and peak-to-median depths for eighteen reference series.

Catalog-scale precision and recall are the measurement that follows that specification. This draft does not contain them.
